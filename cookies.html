Cookies & Local Storage

This app does not use cookies, because it is not a website. It has no browser, no embedded web views for its own content, and no advertising or analytics SDK that could set one.

What it stores on your device:

Your session token, so you are not signed out constantly. Your settings — units, syringe type, whether notifications name the compound. Your favourites and recently-viewed compounds, which stay on the device and are never uploaded. A queue of changes made while offline, held until they can sync. Cached images of your own progress photos.

What it does not store: any identifier used for advertising or tracking, any record of your browsing shared with anyone, any third-party cookie.

Signing out clears your session. Deleting the app removes everything local. Deleting your account removes everything on the server as well.

Following an external link — to PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, or a supplier's certificate — opens your browser, and that site's own cookie policy applies there rather than this one.
