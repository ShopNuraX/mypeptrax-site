Research Use

Most compounds described in this app are not approved for human use.

They are sold for laboratory research, and their labels usually say so. Their presence here is descriptive — a record of what exists and what has been published about it. It is not encouragement, endorsement, or a suggestion that using them is safe, effective, or lawful where you live.

What "research chemical" actually means. No regulator has assessed the compound for human safety. No manufacturer stands behind a dose. Nobody has run the trials that would establish what happens over years. The absence of reported harm is not evidence of safety — it usually means nobody has looked.

Purity is unverified. What is in a vial is what the supplier says is in it. Independent testing of research peptides regularly finds wrong compounds, wrong quantities and contamination. A certificate of analysis is a document from the seller, not an inspection.

The law varies. Possession, import and use are regulated differently in different countries, and some compounds here are prohibited in sport. Checking that is your responsibility.

This app helps you keep records and do arithmetic accurately. It cannot make an unapproved compound safe, and it does not imply that using one is a good idea.
