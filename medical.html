Medical Disclaimer

MyPepTraX does not give medical advice.

Nothing in this app is a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or an instruction to take anything. It is a calculator and a logbook with reference material attached.

The app never chooses a dose for you. Every figure it displays is either one you typed, or one quoted from a named source with that source attached — an approved product label, a registered trial, or a published paper with its identifier. The app does not adjust any figure for your age, weight, sex, health or goals, and will not do so if asked.

Arithmetic is not judgement. The app can tell you that 250 mcg is 10 units at your concentration. It cannot tell you whether 250 mcg is a sensible amount for you, and it does not try.

Speak to a qualified healthcare professional before making any health decision. That applies most strongly where the app has the least to say: compounds with no approval and no clinical record, where nobody has established a human dose and any figure in circulation came from somewhere unverified.

Do not use this app in an emergency. Contact your local emergency service.

If a figure the app shows disagrees with your prescriber or the product in your hand, they are right and the app is wrong. Tell us so it can be fixed.
