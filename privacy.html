MyPepTraX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MyPepTraX is a personal log. This policy describes exactly what it stores and where, and nothing in it is aspirational — each item below corresponds to something the app actually does.

What is stored

Your account is an email address and a password, held by Supabase, our hosting provider. Passwords are hashed by Supabase and are never visible to us or to the app.

Everything you enter is stored against your account: the compounds and vials you record, injections you log with their date, amount and body site, protocols and reminders you create, body measurements, bloodwork results, journal entries, and any progress photos you add.

Creating an account requires a date of birth. It is how the app confirms you are over 21, it is recorded once when the account is created, and it cannot be changed afterwards. It is never used for anything else.

After confirming your email you are asked once for a name, sex and height. These are optional and the form can be skipped; the app works with all of them blank. Sex is recorded so bloodwork can be read against the right reference range. None of it is used to calculate a dose — the calculator uses only the vial, the water added, and an amount you enter yourself — and none of it is shown to anyone else, including in the community, where you appear under a handle you choose separately.

If you send a bug report, it is stored with what you wrote, the app version, your iOS version and your device model, and it is linked to your account so the problem can be looked into. The screen shows you exactly what will be sent before you send it, and nothing from your log, vials or photos is attached.

Progress photos are uploaded to private storage. They are not public, not indexed, and not readable without your session.

Who can see it

Your log is yours alone. Every table enforces row-level security keyed to your user id, so a request carrying your session can read and write your rows and no others. We do not read your log, and there is no admin screen that can display it — not your injections, vials, protocols, measurements, bloodwork, journal or photos.

We do run an administrative console, and it is fair to tell you what it shows: your email address, when you signed up and last signed in, whether you confirmed your email, whether you subscribed, and whether your account or community handle has been suspended. It is used to answer support questions, to moderate the community, and to remove accounts that break the rules. It cannot open any of the health information listed above, and that is enforced by the database rather than by the design of the screen.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no location, no contacts, no health data read from Apple Health. The app contains no advertising SDK.

When the app crashes, Apple's own diagnostics service may later send us the crash: which line of code failed, the app version and the iOS version. It carries no content you entered and nothing that identifies you or your device, it is not linked to your account, and it arrives aggregated on Apple's schedule rather than as it happens. There is no third-party crash or analytics SDK in the app.

Data that leaves your device

Only what is needed to sync your own log to your own account. The compound reference material, research summaries and regulatory data are bundled with the app and are read locally: looking something up sends no request and reveals no interest to anyone.

Where a screen links out to PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov or a supplier's certificate of analysis, following that link is an ordinary web request to that site, subject to their policies rather than ours.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. By default a banner does not name the compound, so a phone face-up on a desk does not disclose what you take. You can change that in Settings.

Your data is yours

Export everything at any time from Settings.

Delete your account from Settings › Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account row, every record listed above and your stored photos are removed. There is no soft-delete and no retained copy to restore from, so export first if you may want it.

Children

MyPepTraX is not intended for anyone under 21, and use requires confirming that age.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new date, and where they affect what is collected you will be asked to review them.

Contact

Questions about this policy, and any request to see, correct, export or delete your data, go to support@mypeptrax.com. Deleting the account from inside the app removes everything immediately and needs no request — Profile, then Delete account.
