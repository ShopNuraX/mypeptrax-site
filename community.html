Community Guidelines

These apply anywhere people can post to each other in this app.

No dosing advice to individuals. Describe your own experience if you want to. Do not tell somebody else what to take, how much, or how often. The difference matters: one is a report, the other is practising medicine on a stranger.

No selling, sourcing or supplier promotion. No vendor links, discount codes, group buys or "DM me". This applies whether or not you profit.

No medical claims you cannot support. Say where a claim comes from. "This cured my injury" is a claim; "I used it for eight weeks and my knee improved" is an experience.

Nothing aimed at minors, and nothing encouraging anyone to use a compound who has not decided to.

No harassment, no doxxing, no bigotry. Disagree with the argument.

Do not repost somebody else's medical information, including screenshots of private messages or bloodwork that is not yours.

Posts that break these are removed. Accounts that repeatedly break them are closed. Report anything you see; blocking a user hides them from you immediately whether or not we act.

We are not moderating for correctness. A post can follow every rule here and still be wrong. Treat it as a stranger's opinion, because that is what it is.
